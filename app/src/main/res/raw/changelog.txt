# 1.0.0

The first release of Sukoon.

• 88 duas from Seeking Serenity across ten sections, from Tranquility to Straight Path
• Reader with Arabic, transliteration and translation, each adjustable and switchable
• Fast, forgiving search across titles, transliteration, translations and references
• Favourites and personal collections
• Tap-anywhere counter for duas recited a set number of times
• Dua of the day, morning and evening reminders, and a home screen widget
• App lock with PIN and biometrics, screenshot blocking and hide from recents
• Backup and restore to a file, light, dark and AMOLED themes, English and Urdu interface
