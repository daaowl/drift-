# Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Sukoon is an offline dua reader. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. In short: nothing leaves your device.

# What Sukoon stores

Sukoon stores these things on your device only: the duas you mark as favourites, the collections you create, your counter progress, your app settings, and, if you turn on App lock, a salted one-way hash of your PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.

# What Sukoon collects

Nothing. Sukoon has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere. If you tap Share on a dua, only that dua's text goes to the app you choose.

# Permissions

Notifications: used only to show the reminders you switch on (dua of the day, morning and evening reminders). Vibration: used for the counter's haptic feedback. Biometrics: used only if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock for App lock. Android handles the biometric check; Sukoon never sees your fingerprint or face data.

# Backups

Sukoon does not upload backups to any cloud service. If you use Settings → Your data → Back up to a file, the app writes a file to a location you choose, and that file is under your control. When you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, your favourites and collections may be copied across; your PIN is not.

# Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Clear my data removes favourites, collections and counters. Uninstalling the app removes everything Sukoon stored.

# Children

Sukoon is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

# Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship inside the app and on the store listing, with a new date above.

# Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact and feedback option in About, or through the developer contact on the store listing.
